How to Send 1099-NEC Forms (due January 31)

If you paid a contractor (for example a 1099 driver or a mechanic who is not a company) enough during the year, you send them a 1099-NEC.

Who gets one

  • Anyone who is not your employee and is not a corporation: 1099 drivers, independent mechanics, helpers.
  • For payments in 2026 onward the limit is $2,000 or more in the year (it was $600 for 2025 and earlier).
  • Employees get a W-2 instead, not a 1099.

When

  • Give the form to the contractor and file it with the IRS by January 31.

What you need

  • A W-9 from each contractor (name, address, SSN or EIN) - ask for it when you hire them
  • Total you paid each one last year (your expenses / driver pay in the Bayloas portal help here)

Steps

  • 1
    Make a list of everyone you paid over the limit last year, with their W-9.
  • 2
    File free online with the IRS IRIS system (link below), or use your accounting software or tax preparer.
  • 3
    Enter your EIN, then each contractor's details and the amount paid.
  • 4
    Send each contractor their copy (email or mail) by January 31.
  • 5
    Keep copies for at least 4 years.
