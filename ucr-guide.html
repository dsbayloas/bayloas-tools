How to Renew Your UCR (Unified Carrier Registration)

Every interstate carrier pays UCR once a year. It takes about 10 minutes online.

What it is

UCR is a yearly federal registration fee for companies that run trucks across state lines. Brokers, shippers and DOT officers can check it, and an unpaid UCR can mean a ticket at a roadside inspection.

When

  • Registration for next year opens October 1.
  • Pay by December 31 - enforcement for the new year starts January 1.

What you need

  • Your USDOT number
  • How many trucks (power units) you run - the fee depends on fleet size
  • A card or bank account to pay

Steps

  • 1
    Go to the official site ucr.gov (only this site - other sites charge extra "service fees").
  • 2
    Click Register and enter your USDOT number.
  • 3
    Check your company details and enter your number of power units.
  • 4
    Pick the year you are paying for (next year) and pay the fee.
  • 5
    Save the receipt (PDF) and upload it to your Bayloas portal: Fleet > Documents > UCR registration.

Open ucr.gov →

Watch out for look-alike sites that email you "UCR renewal" links and charge 2-3x the real fee. Always type ucr.gov yourself.
