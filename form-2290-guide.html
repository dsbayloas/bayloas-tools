How to File Form 2290 (Heavy Vehicle Use Tax)

If your truck is 55,000 lbs or more, you file Form 2290 with the IRS once a year. E-filing gets you the stamped Schedule 1 in minutes.

What it is

A yearly federal tax on heavy trucks (taxable gross weight 55,000 lbs or more). The tax year runs July 1 - June 30. You need the stamped Schedule 1 to renew your plates (IRP).

When

  • Due August 31 for trucks on the road in July.
  • New truck later in the year? File by the end of the month after the month you first used it.
  • Trucks driven 5,000 miles or less in the year (7,500 for farm use) still file, but owe no tax ("suspended").

What you need

  • Your EIN (company tax number - an SSN is not accepted)
  • Each truck's VIN
  • Taxable gross weight of each truck
  • Bank account or card to pay (or pay later through EFTPS)

Steps

  • 1
    Pick an IRS-approved e-file provider (the IRS lists them - link below). Fees are usually small.
  • 2
    Enter your EIN, business name and address.
  • 3
    Add each truck: VIN, taxable gross weight, and the month you first used it this tax year.
  • 4
    Pay the tax: direct debit, EFTPS, card, or check.
  • 5
    Download the stamped Schedule 1 (usually within minutes) and keep it in the truck file.
  • 6
    Upload it to your Bayloas portal: Fleet > Documents > Form 2290.

The most you pay per truck is $550 a year. Late filing adds a penalty and interest - file on time even if you pay a bit later.
