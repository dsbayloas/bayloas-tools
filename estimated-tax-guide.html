How to Pay Quarterly Estimated Tax

Owner-operators don't have tax taken out of each load, so the IRS expects 4 payments during the year. Paying them avoids a penalty in April.

Due dates

PaymentCoversDue
1Jan - MarApril 15
2Apr - MayJune 15
3Jun - AugSeptember 15
4Sep - DecJanuary 15 (next year)

How much to pay

  • Simple safe way: pay 1/4 of last year's total tax each time (110% of it if your income was over $150,000).
  • Or set aside about 25-30% of your profit (gross minus expenses - your Bayloas Money tab shows this) and pay from that.
  • Remember self-employment tax (15.3%) is included in what you owe.
  • Many states also want estimated payments - check your state tax site.

Steps

  • 1
    Work out the amount (see above) - your Money tab in the Bayloas portal shows profit for the quarter.
  • 2
    Go to IRS Direct Pay (free, no signup) - link below.
  • 3
    Choose "Estimated Tax", "1040-ES", and the tax year.
  • 4
    Pay from your bank account and save the confirmation number.
