Your Yearly Tax Return - Checklist for Owner-Operators

Due April 15. Most owner-operators use a tax preparer - this checklist makes it fast and saves deductions.

When

  • File by April 15.
  • Need more time? File an extension (Form 4868) by April 15 - that gives you until October 15 to file, but any tax owed is still due April 15.

Gather these papers

  • Income: broker / factoring statements, 1099s you received (1099-NEC, 1099-K)
  • Your yearly totals from the Bayloas portal: Money > Overview (month view) and Expenses - download the CSV
  • Truck: purchase or lease papers, loan interest, depreciation records
  • Expenses: fuel, repairs, tires, insurance, tolls, permits, phone, ELD, dispatch fees, Form 2290, UCR, IFTA
  • Days away from home (for the per diem deduction)
  • Estimated tax payments you made during the year

Deductions people often miss

  • Per diem: a daily meal allowance for every night away from home (80% deductible for drivers under DOT hours rules) - use our Per Diem tool.
  • Dispatch fees, phone, ELD, load board, parking, scales, truck washes.
  • Health insurance premiums (self-employed).

Steps

  • 1
    Download your yearly loads and expenses from the Bayloas portal (Money tab).
  • 2
    Collect the papers above in one folder.
  • 3
    Take it to your tax preparer, or file yourself (Schedule C for the business, Schedule SE for self-employment tax).
  • 4
    Pay any balance by April 15 with IRS Direct Pay.
