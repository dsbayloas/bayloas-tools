Renewing Insurance, Registration & Other Documents

When a document expires, loads can be held up and brokers stop paying. Here is what to do for each one.

Insurance (COI)

  • 1
    Call your insurance agent 2-3 weeks before the end date to renew.
  • 2
    Make sure the agent files your liability with FMCSA (Form BMC-91/91X) so your authority stays active. Check it on FMCSA's L&I site (link below).
  • 3
    Ask the agent for the new certificate of insurance (COI) and send it to your brokers and factoring company.
  • 4
    Upload the new COI to your Bayloas portal: Fleet > Documents > Insurance (COI) - we send it to brokers for you.

Check your insurance on FMCSA →

Truck registration / IRP plates

  • 1
    Your base state sends a renewal notice about 60 days before.
  • 2
    You need the stamped Form 2290 Schedule 1 and proof of insurance, plus last year's miles by state (your IFTA report has them).
  • 3
    Renew online with your state's IRP office or DMV, pay the fee, and put the new cab card in the truck.
  • 4
    Upload it: Fleet > Trucks > the truck > Registration.

Annual DOT inspection

  • 1
    Every truck and trailer needs an inspection every 12 months.
  • 2
    Book it at a qualified shop or truck stop (many TA/Petro and Love's shops do it).
  • 3
    Keep the report and put the sticker on the truck.
  • 4
    Upload the report: Fleet > Trucks > Annual inspection.

CDL and medical card

  • 1
    Medical card: book a DOT physical with a certified examiner (find one on the FMCSA National Registry - link below). Cards last up to 2 years.
  • 2
    Send the new medical card to your state DMV if your state requires it, so your CDL stays valid.
  • 3
    CDL renewal: through your state DMV before it expires.
  • 4
    Upload both: Fleet > Drivers > the driver.
